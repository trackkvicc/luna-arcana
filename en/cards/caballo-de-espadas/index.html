Knight of Swords tarot card meaning: love, career and advice

Keywords · Uprightambition, fast action, directness
Keywords · Reversedimpulsiveness, aggression

Upright

The Knight of Swords belongs to the realm of the mind, ideas, conflict and truth. It represents decisive action and fast movement. Its keywords: ambition, fast action, directness.

Reversed

Reversed, the card points to impatience or excess in matters of the mind and conflict. Its keywords: impulsiveness, aggression.

Love

In love, applied to the relationship: ambition, fast action, directness.

Career and money

At work: ambition, fast action, directness.

Advice

The key right now: ambition.

Yes or no?

It's a “maybe” card: the answer depends on what you do. Reversed, it leans towards “no”.

Past, present and future

In the past: Some ambition has already shaped your path. Look at what it taught you.
In the present: Right now it's about ambition and fast action. That's the energy of the moment.
In the future: A time of directness is coming. Get ready to welcome it.
