Page of Swords tarot card meaning: love, career and advice

Keywords · Uprightcuriosity, vigilance, new ideas
Keywords · Reversedgossip, haste

Upright

The Page of Swords belongs to the realm of the mind, ideas, conflict and truth. It represents the apprentice: news, curiosity and eagerness to start. Its keywords: curiosity, vigilance, new ideas.

Reversed

Reversed, the card points to immaturity or bad news in matters of the mind and conflict. Its keywords: gossip, haste.

Love

In love, applied to the relationship: curiosity, vigilance, new ideas.

Career and money

At work: curiosity, vigilance, new ideas.

Advice

The key right now: curiosity.

Yes or no?

It's a “yes” card. Reversed, the answer softens to “maybe”.

Past, present and future

In the past: Some curiosity has already shaped your path. Look at what it taught you.
In the present: Right now it's about curiosity and vigilance. That's the energy of the moment.
In the future: A time of new ideas is coming. Get ready to welcome it.
