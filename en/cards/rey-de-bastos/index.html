King of Wands tarot card meaning: love, career and advice

Keywords · Uprightleadership, vision, entrepreneurship
Keywords · Reversedimpulsiveness, arrogance

Upright

The King of Wands belongs to the realm of action, energy, ambition and creativity. It represents outer mastery, mature and firm. Its keywords: leadership, vision, entrepreneurship.

Reversed

Reversed, the card points to authoritarianism or misuse of power in matters of energy and action. Its keywords: impulsiveness, arrogance.

Love

In love, applied to the relationship: leadership, vision, entrepreneurship.

Career and money

Very relevant at work: leadership, vision, entrepreneurship.

Advice

The key right now: leadership.

Yes or no?

It's a “yes” card. Reversed, the answer softens to “maybe”.

Past, present and future

In the past: Some leadership has already shaped your path. Look at what it taught you.
In the present: Right now it's about leadership and vision. That's the energy of the moment.
In the future: A time of entrepreneurship is coming. Get ready to welcome it.
