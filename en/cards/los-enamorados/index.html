The Lovers tarot card meaning: love, career and advice

Keywords · Uprightlove, choice, union, values
Keywords · Reversedimbalance, doubt, wrong choice

Upright

A choice that comes from the heart and from your values. The Lovers speak of union, but also of deciding who you are through what you choose.

Reversed

Indecision or an unbalanced relationship. What you want and what you do don't match.

Love

The love card par excellence: attraction, harmony and deep connection.

Career and money

A partnership or an important decision between two paths.

Advice

Choose what fits what you value.

Yes or no?

It's a “yes” card. Reversed, the answer softens to “maybe”.

Past, present and future

In the past: Some love has already shaped your path. Look at what it taught you.
In the present: Right now it's about love and choice. That's the energy of the moment.
In the future: A time of values is coming. Get ready to welcome it.
